# Clubhouse Studio sound assets

The Studio uses short interface sound effects downloaded from [Mixkit's free interface sound effects library](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/interface/). Mixkit states that these sound effects are free to download and use in projects under its [Mixkit Sound Effects Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree).
